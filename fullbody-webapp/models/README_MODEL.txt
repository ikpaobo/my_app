Free sources for a full-body rigged model:
1) Ready Player Me (export GLB with full body): https://readyplayer.me/
2) Mixamo (auto-rig + download FBX, then convert to GLB with Blender): https://www.mixamo.com/
3) Sketchfab (filter downloadable + rigged): https://sketchfab.com/

Recommended requirements for best tracking:
- Humanoid skeleton (hips, spine, neck, head, upper/lower arms, hands, upper/lower legs, feet)
- Optional finger chains (thumb/index/middle/ring/pinky with 3 joints each)
- Optional facial blendshapes (Smile, JawOpen, EyeBlinkLeft, EyeBlinkRight)
- Texture size 1K or less for low-end PCs
- Mesh under ~50k triangles for smooth FPS
